# Code of conduct

UtilityLab follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@acesonic7](https://github.com/acesonic7), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
